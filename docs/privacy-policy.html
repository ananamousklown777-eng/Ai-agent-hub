Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ask breeze ai, an AI host experience developed as part of AI Agent Hub (“the service”), handles information when you use it.

Information you provide

If you use audience chat, the display name and message you submit are processed to show room messages and generate replies. The current server keeps chat and room state in process memory rather than a persistent application database; this information may be lost when the service restarts. Do not submit passwords, access tokens, payment-card details, confidential information, or sensitive personal information.

Voice and avatar features

If you use voice features, your browser or device may process speech and audio under its own settings and policies. Speech recognition is not guaranteed to be entirely local. If the animated avatar feature is used, session information needed to provide the avatar may be processed by the avatar provider.

TikTok authorization and information

If TikTok Login Kit is enabled and you choose to authorize Ask breeze ai, TikTok may provide the basic profile information permitted by your consent, such as your TikTok user identifier, display name, and avatar. The app configuration also requests the Content Posting API permission to upload content as a draft for you to review and edit in TikTok. The permission does not mean the service can read TikTok LIVE comments or publish directly to your profile. TikTok authorization and API features remain subject to TikTok's approval and the permissions you grant.

The current prototype keeps any TikTok authorization data received by the server in process memory and does not provide durable token storage. Do not assume this prototype is a production-grade credential vault.

Service providers and sharing

Depending on which features you use, message text may be sent to Groq to generate AI replies; session information may be sent to LiveAvatar to provide an animated avatar; Render hosts the service; your browser or device may process voice input and playback; and TikTok handles account authorization and any enabled TikTok API operations. These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. The service does not control their independent practices.

Retention and security

The current application keeps chat state and TikTok authorization data in server memory, and this information may be lost when the service restarts. This does not guarantee that hosting, browser, or third-party providers retain no technical or operational records. The prototype currently has no self-service data export or deletion control and cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to provide a display name or message and can choose not to connect a TikTok account or use voice features. For privacy questions or requests, contact the project through the AI Agent Hub project support page. Do not post passwords, access tokens, or private personal information in a public issue.

Children and policy updates

The service is not designed to collect personal information from children. This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The updated date above indicates the latest revision.
