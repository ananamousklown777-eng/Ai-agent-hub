Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy describes information handling in Breeze Live, an experimental AI-host web prototype.

Information processed

Messages and display names submitted to live chat are processed to show room messages and generate replies. The current server keeps chat history and room messages in memory, not a persistent application database; they may be lost when the service restarts.

If you use voice features, your browser or device may process speech or audio under its own settings and policies. Breeze Live does not promise speech recognition is entirely local.

If you authorize TikTok, TikTok may return an account identifier and authorization tokens. The current server keeps received tokens in process memory and does not provide durable token storage. Do not treat this prototype as a production-grade token vault.

How information is used and shared

Depending on features used, message text may be sent to Groq to generate AI replies; session information may be sent to LiveAvatar to provide an animated avatar; Render hosts the service; and TikTok handles authorization. These providers may process technical or service data under their own terms and policies.

Retention, security, and choices

The prototype stores conversation state and TikTok tokens in server memory. This does not guarantee that hosting or third-party providers retain no technical records. There is currently no self-service export or deletion tool. The service is experimental and cannot guarantee absolute security. Do not submit passwords, access tokens, financial details, sensitive personal information, or confidential information.

Children and policy changes

The service is not designed to collect personal information from children. This policy may be updated when features or practices change.

Contact and privacy requests

Use the AI Agent Hub project contact/issues page. Never post passwords or access tokens in a public issue.
