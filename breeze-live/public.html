Meet Breeze

Ask breeze ai provides an AI host experience with audience-style text chat, AI-generated replies, browser-supported voice input and playback, and an animated digital avatar. Some audience interactions may be simulated for testing.

Open Breeze Live

How it works

Visitors can enter a display name and send a message. The AI backend can generate a reply, and an animated avatar session can speak it when available. Voice features depend on browser and device support.

TikTok integration

TikTok Login Kit lets a user authorize their TikTok account using the user.info.basic permission. Ask breeze ai uses the returned app-specific account identifier to confirm authorization. This revision does not retrieve profile photos or videos, upload or publish videos, or provide a verified feed of real TikTok LIVE comments. Ask breeze ai is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

Privacy and service information

Chat and authorization state are currently held in server memory and may not persist after a restart. Depending on features used, information may be processed by AI, avatar, hosting, browser, and TikTok providers. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, access tokens, or sensitive or confidential information.

The service is an experimental prototype. Features and availability may change.
