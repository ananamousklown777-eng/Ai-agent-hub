Three perspectives, one discussion

  • Quinn proposes a plan and organizes the options.
  • Delta challenges assumptions and highlights risks.
  • Sol turns the discussion into practical next steps.

AI-generated suggestions can be incomplete or wrong. Review the advice and use your own judgment; the Council does not guarantee business results or replace qualified professional advice.

Current availability

This project is still in controlled testing. It is not currently offered for paid purchase. Public registration, checkout, pricing, and automated paid-access delivery have not been established, so this page does not accept orders.

Some assistant access may require a private test key. Do not post or share that key. Do not submit passwords, payment information, personal customer data, or confidential business information.

What testing covers

Automated checks cover selected response and policy rules. Passing tests or seeing a healthy status response does not guarantee that each AI answer is accurate, complete, or available every time.

Before a public paid launch, the project still needs reliable end-to-end live testing, clear pricing and delivery, customer-specific access and usage controls, Council-specific privacy terms, and a support process.
